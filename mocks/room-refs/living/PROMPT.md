## The prompt

Create one photorealistic photograph of a lived-in, upscale Indian living room, shot for an interior design magazine. Wide landscape frame, 16:9, eye level from the room's entrance, a 28mm lens look, everything in sharp focus.

The room: a generous living room in a South Delhi apartment in late-afternoon light. Warm, soft sunlight comes in from tall windows on the left through sheer linen curtains, laying long gentle patches of light across the floor. The walls are a warm sand-coloured lime plaster with a subtle hand-troweled texture. The far wall has a shallow recessed niche framed in pale fluted wood panelling. A deep cove in the ceiling glows with hidden warm LED light. The floor is a matte, honey-toned oak in a herringbone pattern. A large hand-knotted wool rug in muted sand, ivory and faded deep teal sits under the seating. A long, low, curved boucle sofa in oatmeal, with a few linen cushions in deep teal, rust and ivory and a folded throw. A round travertine coffee table. A slim walnut console against the right wall. A tall fiddle-leaf fig in a terracotta pot and a trailing pothos on a shelf. Lived-in details: a stack of two coffee-table books, a small brass tray, a ceramic cup of chai, a soft wool throw over the sofa's arm. Calm, warm, layered, with deep teal and soft gold as the quiet accent colours.

Place these exact products, copied from the attached photos. Reproduce each one faithfully: the same shape, proportions, colours, materials and details. Do not redesign, restyle, simplify or substitute any of them. Each must be clearly visible, uncropped and not hidden behind anything.

1. Photo 1, the gold interlocking-rings LED chandelier: hanging from the centre of the ceiling above the coffee table, switched on with a warm glow.
2. Photo 2, the arc floor lamp with the white drum shade and black base: standing behind the left end of the sofa, its shade arching over the seat, switched on.
3. Photo 3, the framed painting of the horse rider in terracotta, black and blue: hung centred on the wall above the sofa, at the correct large size (122 x 81 cm).
4. Photo 4, the white bull sculpture with gold horns: on the travertine coffee table, beside the books.
5. Photo 5, the black textured vase with brass dogwood branches: on the walnut console, filled with a few fresh stems.
6. Photo 6, the carved soapstone vase: on the floor beside the console, as a standing accent.
7. Photo 7, the gold Hampi stone chariot sculpture: in the lit wall niche, on its own, as a small gallery piece.

Keep the products in their real-world sizes relative to the furniture. Natural shadows and reflections, no text, no watermarks, no people, no extra decor pieces that compete with the seven products.
