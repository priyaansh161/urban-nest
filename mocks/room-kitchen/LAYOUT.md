A typical Noida / Gurugram high-rise kitchen: U-shape, warm wood-laminate tall units and wall cabinets, charcoal-navy base
cabinets, white quartz counter with a white-marble backsplash, a window over the sink, beige glossy vitrified floor, recessed
downlights, under-cabinet LED strips. Day light through the window.

Room 2.9 m wide (x -1.45..1.45) x 3.4 m deep (y -1.7..1.7), ceiling 2.9 m. Blender: x right, y toward the back (window) wall,
z up. Counter top 0.90 m, base cabinets 0.60 deep, wall cabinets 0.35 deep from 1.50 to 2.25 m. Four walls; the FRONT wall
has a wide opening (1.6 m, the way in from the living/dining side, as in the photo), seen as a doorway, no door.
