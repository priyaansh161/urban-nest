Working

How this one works

  1. Pick the piece. Drag the model to set the height and zoom the turn will use — the video starts from whatever angle you leave it at.
  2. Play a preview renders the whole thing in the frame at speed, so you can see it before committing.
  3. Export MP4 captures the turn frame by frame, then records the slideshow to a real MP4 at full resolution. It runs in real time, so a 16-second video takes about 16 seconds plus the capture.
  4. The file lands in Downloads. Upload it to Instagram as a Reel and add music there — the video is silent by design, since Instagram's audio library is where reels get their sound.
