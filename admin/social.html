Working

How this one works

  1. Pick a template first — it decides what's below it and what the piece picker feeds into. Everything else works the same way for all six.
  2. Where there's a 3D piece or scene on stage, drag it to set the height and zoom the capture will use. Where there isn't (Roundup, Montage, Quote, Testimonial), the canvas shows a live still of the current scene instead.
  3. Play a preview renders the whole thing in the frame at speed, so you can see it before committing.
  4. Export MP4 captures whatever needs capturing (a turn, a room) frame by frame, then records the finished video to a real MP4 at full resolution. It runs roughly in real time, so a 16-second video takes about 16 seconds plus the capture.
  5. The file lands in Downloads. Upload it to Instagram as a Reel and add music there — the video is silent by design, since Instagram's audio library is where reels get their sound.
