About "trending"

Instagram does not publish trending hashtags, and its API has no endpoint for them. The nearest thing it offers returns how many posts a hashtag already has, and only for about thirty different hashtags a week per account — not enough to search with. Anything that claims live Instagram trends is either a paid scraper or guessing, so this page does neither.

What it does instead is mix across reach tiers, which is the part that actually matters for an account this size. A post carrying only huge tags is buried within seconds; the niche and local ones are where a small account is genuinely findable. The tiers here are grouped by hand, not measured — treat them as a sensible starting set, edit them freely, and let what actually works guide you. Your own additions are kept in this browser.

The shuffle deliberately changes the mix each time. Posting an identical block of tags on every piece is the pattern most worth avoiding.
