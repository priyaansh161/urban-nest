Article Cards

Turns a Nest post into one Instagram-ready image, plus a caption to go with it — for the feed, not a Reel

Shape
The small marker top-left — "NEW ON THE NEST", "VIRAL", whatever fits. Leave empty for the category.
Shrinks to fit rather than cutting off — a shorter, punchier line reads better than the full SEO title.
Instagram has no way to read text off an image — this is what goes in the caption box when you post it. Edit freely before copying.

How this one works

  1. Pick the article. The photograph, title, category, author and read time all come straight from what's saved in The Nest — nothing to re-type.
  2. Tag and Headline are optional overrides — leave them empty and the card uses the article's own category and title.
  3. Export PNG saves the card to Downloads at the shape you picked. Upload it to Instagram as a normal feed post.
  4. The Caption box drafts the post text — the excerpt, a line pointing to the link in bio, and the article's own tags turned into hashtags. It only redrafts when you press Regenerate, so edits you make stick even if you tweak the Tag or Headline afterward.
