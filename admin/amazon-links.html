Amazon Links

Which of your pieces are still listed on Amazon. Open each link, look at the Amazon page, and mark it. A piece marked Gone stays on Urban Nest but its Amazon button disappears, so nobody lands on a dead listing.

One-time setup needed. The database does not have the Amazon status columns yet. Open Supabase → SQL Editor, paste the contents of admin/add-amazon-status.sql and run it, then reload this page.
How a check works. Press Open ↗. If the Amazon page loads with the product and a Buy option, press Still there. If it says "page not found", "currently unavailable" with no way to buy it, or shows a different product, press Gone. Links are done in order of oldest check first; anything older than 30 days comes back for a re-check. Links that are only amazon.in with no product (like the Bathroom MK1 dots) are listed separately — there is nothing to check until a real product link is pasted in.
