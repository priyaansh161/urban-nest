Amazon Links

Which of your pieces are still listed on Amazon. Open each link, look at the Amazon page, and mark it. A piece marked Gone stays on Urban Nest but its Amazon button disappears, so nobody lands on a dead listing.

One-time setup needed. The database does not have the Amazon status columns yet. Open Supabase → SQL Editor, paste the contents of admin/add-amazon-status.sql and run it, then reload this page.
How a check works. Press Open ↗. If the Amazon page loads with the product and a Buy option, press Still there. If it says "page not found", "currently unavailable" with no way to buy it, or shows a different product, press Gone. Links are done in order of oldest check first; anything older than 30 days comes back for a re-check.
Once a month: Run full check. It opens every link for you, one at a time (about 10 minutes, keep the page open), marks the clear ones Still there or Gone, lists pieces whose Amazon price has changed with an Update price button, and leaves anything unclear for you to look at. Links that are only amazon.in with no product (like the Bathroom MK1 dots) are listed separately — there is nothing to check until a real product link is pasted in.
