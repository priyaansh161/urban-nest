How to record it

  1. Pick the piece, the ground and the shape. Drag the model to choose the angle it starts from — height and zoom are yours to set.
  2. Hit Start recording mode. Everything but the frame disappears and the piece begins turning.
  3. Press Win + G for the Windows Game Bar, then its record button. Or use OBS if you have it.
  4. Let it turn at least one full turn past the countdown, then stop. Trim the countdown off in Instagram.
  5. Esc to come back. Files land in Videos\Captures.
