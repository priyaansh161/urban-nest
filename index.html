THE URBAN NEST IDEA

It's the little things
that make a home
a home.

Home décor in India has long been treated as a luxury for the few — expensive chandeliers, lakh-worth furniture, inaccessible aesthetics. We disagree.

It's the soap dispenser, the bath rug, the cotton jar on your countertop. The small, intentional choices that quietly transform your everyday space into something beautiful. Urban Nest exists to make that possible for everyone.
