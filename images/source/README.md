# Source images (not deployed)

Drop **full-resolution originals** here — the biggest version you can get.
Filenames don't matter.

Nothing in this folder is served to the site. Optimised WebP variants are
generated from these into `images/` and those are what the pages load, so a
40 MB original here costs visitors nothing.

## What's needed

| Use | Orientation | Minimum width |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| Homepage hero (desktop) | landscape, 16:9 or wider | 2560 px |
| Homepage hero (mobile)  | portrait                 | 1400 px |
| Room cards              | portrait, near 4:5       | 1200 px |
| Nest article headers    | landscape                | 1600 px |

## What reads as premium here

Rooms rather than product still-lifes. Natural light. Uncluttered. Warm
neutrals — cream, oak, stone, terracotta — since strong blues and greens
fight the gold. Leave a calm area of wall or floor where a headline can sit
and stay legible.
