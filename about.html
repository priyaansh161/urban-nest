WHO WE ARE

We believe your home should feel like you.

OUR NEST

Where the idea began

Urban Nest was born from a frustration that many Indian households quietly share — beautiful, functional home products are everywhere, but finding them feels impossible. Endless scrolling, inconsistent quality, and no sense of how things actually look in a real space.

We noticed something else too. Home décor in India has long been treated as a luxury for the few — expensive chandeliers, lakh-worth furniture, inaccessible aesthetics. The middle-class Indian home was left out of the conversation.

We disagreed. It's the small things — a soap dispenser, a bath rug, a cotton jar on your countertop — the intentional little choices that quietly transform an ordinary apartment into something genuinely beautiful. You should not need a five-lakh budget to feel at home.

Urban Nest was built to bridge that gap. Not a store. Not a catalogue. A curated living experience — where you walk into a room, see how it feels, and discover the pieces that make it yours.

OUR MISSION

Affordable luxury.
For real homes.

Our mission is to introduce affordable luxury that uplifts the standard of living of the everyday Indian household — one intentional object at a time.

We want to give the Indian consumer a sense of prestige through the ordinary. To show that this is the normal — that living beautifully is not a privilege, it is a choice that everyone can make.

Every product on Urban Nest is chosen with intention. Functional over decorative. Timeless over trendy. Affordable without feeling cheap.

OUR VISION

The Experience Nest

We envision Urban Nest as India's most trusted lifestyle curation platform — a space where home décor, kitchen essentials, bedding, personal care, and décor all live under one beautifully curated roof.

Online first, we are building a brand and an experience. Eventually, Urban Nest will move into physical concept stores — Experience Nests — in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities, designed not as traditional retail but as fully realized room environments where customers walk through curated spaces and discover products the way they were meant to be seen.

Because the Indian consumer deserves to touch, feel, and truly understand what they are bringing home.
