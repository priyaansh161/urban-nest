OUR NEST

Where the idea began

Urban Nest was born from a frustration that many Indian households quietly share. Beautiful, functional home products are everywhere, but finding them feels impossible. Endless scrolling, inconsistent quality, and no sense of how things actually look in a real space.

We noticed something else too. Home décor in India has long been treated as a luxury for the few: expensive chandeliers, lakh-worth furniture, inaccessible aesthetics. The middle-class Indian home was left out of the conversation.

We disagreed. It's the small things. A soap dispenser, a bath rug, a cotton jar on your countertop. The intentional little choices that quietly transform an ordinary apartment into something genuinely beautiful. You should not need a five-lakh budget to feel at home.

Urban Nest was built to bridge that gap. Not a store. Not a catalogue. A curated living experience, where you walk into a room, see how it feels, and discover the pieces that make it yours.

"The home should be the treasure chest of living." Le Corbusier
OUR MISSION

Affordable luxury.
For real homes.

Our mission is to introduce affordable luxury that uplifts the standard of living of the everyday Indian household, one intentional object at a time.

We want to give the Indian consumer a sense of prestige through the ordinary. To show that this is the normal: living beautifully is not a privilege, it is a choice that everyone can make.

Every product on Urban Nest is chosen with intention. Functional over decorative. Timeless over trendy. Affordable without feeling cheap.

"Form ever follows function." Louis Sullivan
OUR VISION

The Experience Nest

We envision Urban Nest as India's most trusted lifestyle curation platform, a space where home décor, kitchen essentials, bedding, personal care, and décor all live under one beautifully curated roof.

Online first, we are building a brand and an experience. Eventually, Urban Nest will move into physical concept stores, Experience Nests, in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities, designed not as traditional retail but as fully realized room environments where customers walk through curated spaces and discover products the way they were meant to be seen.

Because the Indian consumer deserves to touch, feel, and truly understand what they are bringing home.

ON THE RENDERS

What you are
looking at.

Every piece on Urban Nest is shown as a 3D model, built with AI assistance from the maker's own photographs. Nothing you see is a photograph of the object itself, and none of it was shot by us.

We do it this way because a photograph makes one decision on your behalf and then refuses to discuss it. One angle, one light, one distance. A chandelier is bought on detail — the cut of the glass, how the arms sit against each other, whether the drops read as crystal or as plastic — and a single flattering frame is exactly where that detail goes to hide. A model lets you turn the thing over, put it against a dark ground or a pale one, and look for as long as you like before deciding.

The honest limit of it: a model is a close likeness, not a measurement. Colour is the first thing to drift, particularly under your own lighting. Finish and texture are next — the difference between brushed and polished brass survives the process badly. Small proportions can shift, and a render carries no sense of weight in the hand at all.

What does not drift: every piece is modelled on a real product you can actually buy. The specification table on each piece, and the retailer's own page behind the shop link, are the facts. Where the render and the specification disagree, the specification is right — and if the two of them disagree with what arrives at your door, we would genuinely like to hear about it.

We would rather say all of this plainly than let a beautiful render quietly do the arguing. Treat what you see as a good likeness of the thing, and the numbers underneath it as the thing itself.
